## Proposed product change (conditional)

Use one internal `RECEIVER_RPC_LIVENESS_MS = 30000` constant in the receiver client and receiver service. Keep the general IPC default at 15000 and its existing 1..900000 validator, public controller's explicit 300000, and clock service's explicit 5000 unchanged. Receiver endpoints do not accept an untrusted caller-selected budget. No environment override is added.

This is an explicit product behavior change: a locally authenticated receiver request may wait for at most the new liveness deadline instead of failing at the inherited 15-second default. It is not a test-only setting. The 30000 proposal is conditional on complete measured work fitting it with headroom; reject or revise the design if measurement contradicts that premise.

All authority clocks, `notBefore`/`validUntil`, material `expiresAt:105000`, principal/peer checks, immutable tuples and signatures, cancellation, stock custody, fresh complete Current/SQL census and closing fences stay byte-identical. A longer connection lifetime must not make expired, revoked, cancelled, partial, or unknown work successful. No caching, retry, replay, owner removal, population sampling, early ACK or fabricated physical completion is permitted.

## Resource and adversarial analysis

Admission remains four sockets with the same frame limit, peer authentication and close/drain path. The worst nominal occupancy rises from four times 15 to four times 30 socket-seconds: a bounded twofold increase, not a 20-fold controller-wide increase. This tradeoff must be recorded rather than described as free. The service already requires the configured local controller UID; no unauthenticated access or cross-user privilege is added. Existing synchronous callbacks cannot be preempted by either timeout: the original monotonic checks after work must still reject after the deadline. Unknown physical outcomes remain unknown and may retain resources; raising the deadline is never evidence of reclamation.

Adversarial cases: a peer that connects and never responds; a before-send callback that blocks beyond 30 seconds; caller cancellation before the deadline; late response after clock expiry; revocation inside a Current callback; a changed SQL schema/function/native construction surface; parent references made stale by child republication. Existing qualification checks for these facts remain mandatory.
