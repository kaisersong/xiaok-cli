# Intent dependency test observation windows

This change affects tests only. It creates no Source, scope, authority, Native qualification or physical-close proof.

The original release plan explicitly permits quality to determine reasonable CI runner budgets. On the unchanged original Intent test file, local Node 24 completed 22 cases with one existing opt-in skip in 759 seconds. Hosted macOS Node 24 cancelled eight cases at their 30–50-second Vitest windows; the first case took about 34.6 seconds locally versus 49.3 seconds in CI against its 45-second runner limit. The preserved logs are failures, not passes.

The case observer cancellation is separate from the original qualified authority clock, material expiry and transport limits. `f.setTime(10000)`, material `expiresAt:105000`, actual RPC limits, original Current/whole-population checks, signals, late-error propagation and real cleanup joins remain unchanged. `onTestFinished` still waits for the original scenario. Unknown cleanup never becomes a successful completion.

Only cases that construct the full fixture inside their bodies use a reviewed macOS observer minimum: 60 seconds on Node 22/23 and 90 seconds on Node 24 or later. Other operating systems retain their original budgets. Longer original budgets remain, including the 105-second republishing case and the one-hour opt-in case. The five invocation cases already construct their fixtures in `beforeEach`; their case budgets stay unchanged. `beforeEach` remains 90 seconds and `afterEach` 30 seconds.

The original 33,708-byte file was not exempt from the 20KB rule. Its fixture functions moved to adjacent `intent-publication-dependency-fixture.ts`; the final completion cases moved to `intent-completion-dependencies.test.ts`. Both test files retain the original file-local hoisted state and two mocks. The fixture module adds no mutable module-level state. Vitest forks retain their original isolation.
