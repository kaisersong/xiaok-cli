## Hosted evidence correction and blocking coverage

The b886 workflow inherited `continue-on-error: true` for Intent execution. Job success was not test success: actual macOS Node22 shard2 had six failures and Node24 shard2 had four failures, with test windows exceeded and real controller abort errors. These remain failures. The split also left the five completion cases outside the workflow's single-file collection. The earlier claim of both hosted Intent groups passing is withdrawn.

The workflow now collects, runs and audits both files in separate directories for every OS/Node/shard, aggregates eight distinct file/platform groups (24 manifests), and treats either real runner's nonzero exit as a blocking job failure. Both runs and audits execute independently after successful collection, including after another run fails. The original nine blocking source suites remain. Generic coverage auditing still records failed outcomes truthfully; no test failure is accepted as a release pass.

Independent Kimi approved the correction design and the macOS 300-second runner minimum based on actual hosted costs and multi-fixture cases. Only full fixtures constructed inside the case use it; five invocation cases' existing setup/runner boundaries and every production/SDK clock, signal and join remain unchanged. Job and per-run observation ceilings are 45/30 minutes. No cancellation, unknown close or late error becomes success.

Final b886 local minimum Node22.14 complete two-file execution passed 22 mandatory cases with one original opt-in skip (exit0, 750.88s). This local pass is distinct from the failed hosted attempts. The corrected final complete SHA must execute and pass both files in CI before release.
